Get it fixed

Bring it in to the shop

Almost every fault needs to be seen in person, so the best thing to do is bring it to the shop. We'll take a look and tell you what's wrong and what it will cost before we touch it.

  1. 1

    Drop it in

    Bring it to the shop on Longforth Road during opening hours. You'll find us on the Shell garage forecourt.

  2. 2

    We take a look

    We find the fault and give you a price before any work starts. Nothing is done without your go-ahead.

  3. 3

    Collect it fixed

    Many phone repairs are ready in about an hour. Computers are typically back with you in 1–3 days.

What to bring

  • The device, plus its charger or power lead
  • Your passcode, PIN or login, so we can test it properly
  • Any accessories involved, like a case or memory card
  • If it still works, a backup of anything you can't lose
